Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about originals, commissions, and care.

Q: How can I verify the authenticity of an original painting from MysticArtStudio?

Answer: Every original piece from MysticArtStudio is accompanied by a hand-signed Certificate of Authenticity (COA).

Q: What should I look for when identifying a trustworthy website to buy art online?

Answer: A trustworthy art platform should provide transparent artist bios, high-resolution zoom capabilities (on request) and secure, encrypted payment gateways.

Q: How do I commission a custom painting from a professional artist?

Answer: Commissioning art at MysticArtStudio is a collaborative 4-step journey:

  1. Consultation: We discuss your vision, color palette, and space requirements.
  2. Concept Sketch: The artist provides a preliminary draft for your approval.
  3. Creation: You receive mid-process updates (photos/videos)
  4. Final Reveal: The completed masterpiece is varnished, framed (if requested additionally), and shipped.
Q: How much does a commissioned painting cost, and how is the price determined?

Answer: Commission prices are calculated based on three factors: dimensions (size), complexity of the subject matter, and materials used. We provide a transparent, upfront quote after the initial consultation. Typically, a 50% deposit secures your place in the artist's schedule, with the remaining balance due upon completion.

Q: What is the best way to preserve and clean an original oil or acrylic painting?

Answer: Avoid hanging art in direct sunlight or high-moisture areas like bathrooms. To clean, use a dry, soft-bristled natural hair brush (like a clean makeup brush) to gently whisk away dust. Never use water, glass cleaner, or solvents, as these can react with the pigment or varnish. For deep restoration, consult a professional.

Q: Why should I invest in original art rather than mass-produced prints?

Answer: Original art carries the "artist’s hand"—the unique texture, depth, and energy that a flat print cannot replicate. Beyond aesthetics, original works are appreciating assets.
