FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Mystic Art Studio

Welcome! Here are answers to common questions about our art, brand services, custom orders, merchandise, pricing, and how to work with us.

How can I verify the authenticity of an original painting from Mystic Art Studio?

Every original piece from Mystic Art Studio includes a hand-signed Certificate of Authenticity (COA).

What should I look for when buying art online?

Look for clear artist bios, high-resolution artwork previews, and secure, encrypted payment options.

How do I commission a custom painting?

Start with a consultation to share your vision and space details, then approve a concept sketch before the final piece is created.

How is the price of a commissioned painting determined?

Pricing is based on size, subject complexity, and materials, with a clear quote provided after the initial consultation.

How do I preserve and clean an original painting?

Keep artwork away from direct sunlight and humidity, and dust gently with a dry, soft-bristled brush.

Why invest in original art instead of mass-produced prints?

Original art carries the artist’s hand, texture, and energy, and often holds long-term value beyond decor.

Can I request a custom artwork?

Yes — describe your idea in the contact form or book a consultation with the artist.

How is merchandise shipped?

We use tracked shipping within India and internationally — typical delivery is 7–14 business days.
