Where Art Meets Strategy. Where Stories Become Brands.

By Yamuna Padmanaban

I'm Yamuna—a career marketer and art therapist who believes art wins hearts much before logic steps in.

I blend imagination with insight to craft brand narratives that connect, inspire, and endure. Whether building soulful brands or guiding inner healing through art, my work bridges purpose and expression. Every canvas, every campaign—designed to spark connection and meaning.

This is where creativity becomes clarity—and stories come alive.

The studio

I blend brand strategy with original artwork. Every project begins with purpose—I listen for the emotion behind the brief and translate it into visuals that feel alive. From brand storytelling to collectible art, the work is guided by clarity, craft, and soulful expression.

What I create

Visual identities, brand narratives, and meaningful art objects that hold memory and meaning. A brand story told through design. A hand-painted product line. A commissioned artwork. Each piece—an experience that invites people to feel, remember, and connect.

Who it's for

Founders, brand leaders, and art collectors who want more than surface-level aesthetics. If you're building a brand with soul, creating a legacy of art, or looking for a gift that tells a story—I'd love to bring your vision to life.

How I work

Each collaboration is rooted in curiosity and empathy. I explore the brand values, the audience it serves, and the emotional tone it needs to carry. The result? Artwork and branding that feels intentional, poetic, and rooted in authenticity. The process is calm, thoughtful, and tailored to your pace.

Created slowly for a fast world.
