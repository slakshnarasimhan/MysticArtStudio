Where Art Meets Strategy. Where Stories Become Brands.

Mystic Art Studio is the creative universe of Yamuna Padmanaban—a career marketer and art therapist who blends imagination with insight to craft powerful brand narratives. With a rare fusion of art, emotion, and strategy, Yamuna transforms ideas into stories that connect, inspire, and endure. Whether building soulful brands or guiding inner healing through art, her work bridges purpose and expression. Every canvas, campaign, and concept is designed to spark connection and meaning. This is where creativity becomes clarity—and stories come alive.

MysticArtStudio at a glance

MysticArtStudio is an art brand and creative studio that blends brand strategy with original artwork. Every project begins with purpose: we listen for the emotion behind the brief and translate it into visuals that feel alive. From brand storytelling to collectible art, the work is guided by clarity, craft, and soulful expression.

What we create

We create visual identities, brand narratives, and meaningful art objects that hold memory and meaning. Whether it is a brand story told through design, a hand-painted product line, or a commissioned artwork, the studio shapes experiences that invite people to feel, remember, and connect.

Who it is for

MysticArtStudio serves founders, brand leaders, and art collectors who want more than surface-level aesthetics. If you are building a brand with soul, creating a legacy of art, or looking for a gift that tells a story, this is a studio that can bring your vision to life.

How we work

Each collaboration is rooted in curiosity and empathy. We explore the brand values, the audience it serves, and the emotional tone it needs to carry. The result is artwork and branding that feels intentional, poetic, and rooted in authenticity. The process is calm, thoughtful, and tailored to your pace.
