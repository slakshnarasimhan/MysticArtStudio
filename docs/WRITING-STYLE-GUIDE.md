# MysticArtStudio Writing Style Guide

A reference for content that matches Yamuna Padmanaban's voice across the Artbrush / MysticArtStudio website. Based on analysis of her blog posts at [MysticArtStudio Blog](https://slakshnarasimhan.github.io/MysticArtStudio/blog.html).

---

## Tone & Voice

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Warm & personal** | First-person, reflective, conversational — like talking to a thoughtful friend |
| **Inviting** | Ends with questions or calls to action to engage the reader |
| **Encouraging** | Supportive, not preachy — "you can do this" energy |
| **Soulful** | Connects emotion with craft; art and intention go together |

---

## Structure

### Openings
- Start with a **provocative question** or **bold statement**
- Examples: "Do you know even machines need space to be Creative :) ?", "The day I stopped 'Follow'ing others .. was the day my real brand was born!", "Do you remember your first sale?"

### Paragraphs
- **Short** — often one or two sentences
- Single idea per paragraph

### Section headers
- Use clear labels: "The background:", "The approach:", "The result?", "The takeaway:"
- Helps readers scan and follow the narrative

### Bullet points
- Use for lists, takeaways, and key points
- Em dashes (—) or hyphens for list items

---

## Language & Punctuation

| Element | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| **Em dashes (—)** | For asides, emphasis, and parenthetical thoughts |
| **Ellipses (...)** | For pauses, reflection, or trailing thought |
| **Exclamation marks** | Sparingly, for genuine enthusiasm |
| **Parentheses** | For quick clarifications or asides |
| **Smileys** | Occasional, light touch (e.g. :) ) |
| **Spaced letters** | Rarely, for emphasis (e.g. f .r .e .e .d .o .m) |

---

## Vocabulary

### Preferred words & phrases
- soulful, authentic, intentional, meaningful
- storytelling, narrative, story
- gentle, quiet, heartfelt
- "Art wins hearts much before logic steps in"
- "authentic expression" over "amplification"
- impressions vs imprints (tangible vs fleeting)

### Themes to weave in
- Art meets business / marketing
- Authenticity over trends
- Personal journey + professional insight
- Community, belief, connection
- Tangible objects that carry meaning

---

## Endings

- **Question** — invite the reader to reflect or respond
- **Call to action** — "Let's talk!", "Would you be that first believer?"
- **Takeaway** — one clear lesson or thought
- **"Here's to…"** — celebratory close when appropriate
- **Hashtags** — optional for social-style posts (#personalbranding, #authenticity)

## Quick checklist for new content

- [ ] Opens with a hook (question or bold statement)
- [ ] Uses first-person where appropriate
- [ ] Short paragraphs, one idea each
- [ ] Em dashes and ellipses for rhythm
- [ ] Soulful, authentic vocabulary
- [ ] Ends with invitation or takeaway
- [ ] Warm, not corporate or stiff
