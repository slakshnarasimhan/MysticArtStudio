The day I stopped 'Follow'ing others .. was the day my real brand was born!

For years in marketing, I focused on tracking trends, studying data and following best practices — that’s what success meant to me.
….Until I started my art business @mysticartstudio and my own art collection.

Like any good student, I began by studying the masters — observing their strokes, color palettes, and techniques. 

But somewhere along the way, something was missing ..especially with something like art which is almost like a self-reflection!

And there was the profound truth:
The first step to creating something truly mine was to unlearn what was already out there!

I paused chasing “what people like” or “what people buy” and started asking,
“What subjects do I resonate with?”
 “What story do I want to tell?”
— that’s when my work began to feel alive.

Because every artwork carries a piece of the artist.
Not just in the hours spent, but in the choices — the colors, the strokes, the subjects.

In marketing or in art, it’s easy to copy what’s trending….But if you’re building a personal brand, your real power lies in the quiet work of self-discovery — understanding what moves you, not what moves the algorithm.

My biggest learning?
“ Art — and branding — both require patience and consistency.
To listen inwardly, and to create outwardly from that place of truth “

Authenticity isn’t a buzzword. It’s a practice.
And it takes hundreds of iterations ( literally 100s of artworks) before your voice — or your brushstroke — finally feels like you.

Whether you’re an artist, a marketer, or a creator — the most magnetic brand you can build is the one that resonates with your soul first.
 I would love to hear from fellow creators — what moment made you realise that your voice was different from the noise?
