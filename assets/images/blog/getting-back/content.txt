What had changed when I returned to corporate life ….after I quit my job to become an artist for some years?

Everything!

I wasn’t coming back as an employee—I came back as an entrepreneur after building my own little startup :) And that made all the difference.

I wasn't just making art—I took up formal art education, started building my brand, my business, thinking about growth, choosing the right projects, deciding where to invest, constantly learning new skills and building my art network

No KPIs. No manager. No appraisals.
Just free will—and a fierce amount of discipline. I became my own mentor and manager :) 

Sure, I missed the casual office banter on grey days.
But I built a rhythm: clocking into my studio every morning, taking lunch breaks like I was in a boardroom, wrapping up only when it was time for school pickup.

Running your own thing isn’t all passion and paint—it’s perseverance, process, and patience.

Every art show, every sale was a celebration.
But it was a solo party—no team huddles, no office cake.
Just me, smiling quietly in gratitude.

But the biggest reward? I never needed a break from work. My art was my break.

 Today, I look back at this chapter with so much bliss. 
🔹 150+ artworks sold 🔹 Corporate art workshops 🔹 Launched Mystic Art Bags as a product line 🔹 Built a full-fledged studio from scratch 🔹Art shows and Recognitions across states 🔹Articles in Newspapers and SoMe

It wasn’t easy.
But it was mine.
And it was worth every moment.
And No, it wasn’t a career break — it turned out to be my career accelerator 

What mattered was ‘to do whatever you do with passion!’

Here’s to choosing yourself.
Here’s to creative leaps.
Here’s to reinvention. 💫
