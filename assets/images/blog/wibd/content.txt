Art has an unique advantage — one that strong brands continually aspire to -
 it communicates meaning instantly !

The background : 
As WiBD marked 10 years of impact, the challenge was clear—how do you capture the essence of a decade long movement in a way that feels authentic, human, and memorable?

The approach : 
As the Event & Gifting Partner, I chose art as the storytelling medium—subtle in form, strong in intent.
- Folk art as the style for its simplicity and universal accessibility
- The Kalpavriksha—the mythical tree —as the central metaphor ! - A living symbol of a community that grows by nurturing women talent, mentoring with intent, and being replenished by those it empowers
- A humble jute bag as the canvas—so the story could travel home with every member
- Thoughtful colours and details aligned to the theme and brand ethos

The result?
 -An effortless, emotional connection—because the story was felt.

The takeaway :
I see this as a success story of ‘gentle’ art building a stronger brand narrative.
Because:
* Art wins hearts much before logic steps in

* Visual storytelling is embedded in human evolution

 * Complex brand stories can be distilled into memorable moments

* Emotion creates originality—and originality builds trust

* In a crowded marketplace, visual stories cut through noise

* And true differentiation goes far beyond logos and taglines
