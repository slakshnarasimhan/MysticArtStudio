Event Partner & Volunteer Spotlight

Excited to shine a spotlight on the incredibly talented Yamuna Padmanaban—artist, event partner, and volunteer for the upcoming Women in Big Data India Bengaluru Summit on September 19!

Yamuna’s painting, “The Kalpavriksha of Connections,” beautifully embodies the spirit of Women in Big Data, symbolizing the deep roots of mentorship, the flourishing growth of women’s talent, and the continuous cycle of leadership that defines our community. We’re excited to feature her artistic talent on the exclusive summit swag, designed specially for this milestone event for all our attendees. Yamuna’s thoughtful design is a celebration of our community's connection and growth.
