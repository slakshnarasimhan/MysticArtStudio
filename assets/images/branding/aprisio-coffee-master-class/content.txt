About the experience
An engaging Coffee Masterclass, where guests will embark on an interactive journey through the world of specialty coffee with expertly crafted brews and live demonstrations.

Join us for an immersive Coffee Masterclass - exclusively for Aprisio Members

Aprisio has partnered with GoldRush Coffee - India’s first performance Coffee - to bring this Masterclass to you.



What is included:

Coffee Education: Understanding sourcing, processing, roasting, and brewing methods.
Hands-On Brewing: A dedicated setup with French Press, Espresso Machine and V60, allowing guests to actively participate in the brewing process under expert guidance.
Live Demonstrations: Our expert barista will showcase brewing techniques, discussing origin, grind size, method and flavor profiles for each brewing style.
Tasting & Nibbles: Guests will be served a curated selection of ten hi-tea dishes paired with a refreshing cold brew, enhancing the tasting experience alongside freshly brewed specialty coffee.
A Coffee themed Aprisio goodie bag.

What's not:

Food & drinks from the Club restaurants, bars & pantry

Dress Code: SMART CASUALS. FOR MEN - COLLARED SHIRTS & T-SHIRTS, TROUSERS/JEANS & CLOSED SHOES. SHORTS, ROUND NECK SHIRTS & T-SHIRTS & OPEN TOED SHOES & SANDALS STRICTLY NOT PERMITTED.
