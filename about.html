About the Artist

Yamuna Padmanaban

By Yamuna Padmanaban

Yamuna Padmanaban, the artist behind Mystic Art Studio, is known for her versatile and thoughtful creations. Her work ranges from striking portraits to customized commissioned pieces, showcasing her ability to connect deeply with her subjects and clients.
