About the Artist

Yamuna Padmanaban

By Yamuna Padmanaban

Yamuna Padmanaban is an accomplished artist and certified art therapist, holding a Diploma in Art Therapy and an Advanced Diploma in Painting from the Karnataka Chitrakala Parishath. She is the creative force behind Mystic Art Studio, with work that spans striking portraits and personalized commissioned pieces, each reflecting her deep connection with subjects and clients. Yamuna has participated in multiple state and national art shows, showcasing her versatility and artistic vision. Beyond exhibitions, she has conducted numerous art therapy sessions and consultations, combining her expertise in communications and visual arts to help individuals express, reflect, and heal—where every brushstroke speaks volumes beyond words.
