A Safe Space to Pause, Reflect, and Create

This guided art therapy practice is designed to help you slow down, reconnect with yourself, and gently explore your inner world through creative expression. You do not need to be an artist. This is a mindful, therapeutic process that focuses on feeling, not perfection.

Created by Yamuna Padmanaban, a career marketer and certified art therapist, this practice blends emotional awareness with creative flow. It offers a simple, structured way to release stress, gain clarity, and reconnect with your intuition.

What You will Experience

  • A calming, step-by-step art therapy exercise
  • Gentle prompts to explore emotions and thoughts
  • A safe, judgment-free creative space
  • Improved emotional clarity and self-awareness
  • A sense of grounding, calm, and release

Who Is This Practice For?

This free art therapy worksheet is ideal for:

  • Adults experiencing stress, anxiety, or burnout
  • Anyone navigating change, grief, or emotional overwhelm
  • Creatives feeling blocked or disconnected
  • Professionals needing a mindful reset
  • Anyone curious about art as a tool for healing

No prior art experience is required, just an open heart and a few simple materials.

How to Use the Worksheet

  1. Find a quiet, comfortable space.
  2. Set aside 20–30 minutes without distractions.
  3. Follow the guided prompts in the worksheet.
  4. Create freely. There is no right or wrong.
  5. Reflect gently on what emerges.

This is your time to breathe, express, and reconnect.
