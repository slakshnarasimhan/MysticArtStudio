Gift Art

Originals under ₹2000

By Yamuna Padmanaban

The Gift Art page is an accessible catalog of original paintings by Yamuna Padmanaban in Bengaluru, India. Each listed artwork is priced at INR 2000 and available through a direct inquiry workflow. Buyers can review title, visual style, and emotional context before requesting purchase confirmation.

Key Questions Answered

What is the Gift Art collection?

Gift Art is a curated set of original hand-painted artworks offered at a fixed INR 2000 price point. The collection is designed for accessible art purchases and meaningful gifting.

How does buying from the Gift Art page work?

Buyers review artwork details and use the Contact to Buy action. The studio confirms availability and shares next purchase steps directly.

When should someone choose Gift Art instead of custom commissions?

Gift Art is appropriate when buyers prefer immediate selection and fixed pricing. Commissioned work is more suitable for custom themes, sizes, or brand-specific concepts.

Why is Gift Art priced at a single rate?

Single-rate pricing reduces decision friction for first-time art buyers. The pricing model also improves transparency across all listings.

Best practices for selecting a gift artwork?

Match artwork mood and color palette to the recipient or interior context. Check display size requirements and framing preference before finalizing.
